# I open-sourced 30 pure-code styles for knowledge short videos

Tired of the same talking-head face, the same AI slideshow transitions, the same “big quote on screen”?

I wanted the opposite: **no talking head, no brand logo, one continuous BGM**, and a frame that never sits still — knowledge as motion design.

**vibe-knowledge** (MIT): pick a topic + pick a style → render a ~2‑minute knowledge short in pure code.

Repo: https://github.com/maomyu/vibe-knowledge

## Why “pure code”, not another template pack

- **skia-python → ffmpeg**: every frame is drawn, not dragged on a timeline.
- **Style = visual grammar**: templates hold no topic copy; text/rhythm/data live in `script.json`.
- **Hard rules in the skill**: zero stillness, one motion per line, one primary caption, bottom captions ≥2s (readable first), music-locked cuts, anti-strobe.
- **Review before render**: full copy approved first, then pixels.

It’s not “Remotion with vibes”. It’s the information density of Douyin knowledge clips, turned into a reproducible render spec.

## The line

1. Topic (human nature / crowd psychology / sticky effects)
2. Full copy review (~520–580 CJK chars, ~115–130s cut)
3. `script.json` per style schema
4. Render + density check (longest static target: 0s)
5. Ship

Templates, schema, richness standard are in the repo. **Full BGM is not redistributed** (bring your own cleared track).
